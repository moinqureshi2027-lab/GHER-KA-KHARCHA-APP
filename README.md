# Ghar Kharcha 🏠

Ghar Kharcha is a beautiful 3D home expense manager designed for housewives who want complete control over the household budget — in simple Roman Urdu, right from their phone.

Managing ghar ka kharcha shouldn't need a notebook or complicated spreadsheets. Ghar Kharcha puts everything in one place: daily expenses, monthly income, fixed bills, savings goals, charity (sadqa), and weekly savings tracking — all wrapped in a gorgeous 3D mobile-first design with a plum-rose gradient, gold accents, glossy cards, and big touch-friendly buttons.

When you open the app for the first time, a friendly 3-step setup asks for your monthly income, monthly budget, and your first expense — and you're ready in under a minute.

The Home screen is your command center: a Bachat hero card shows exactly how much you've saved, a budget progress bar warns you in amber at 80% and turns red with a "Budget cross ho gaya" alert at 100%, plus today's spending, top categories, a Sadqa card, and this week's savings at a glance.

The Kharcha tab handles daily life — 16 categories from Ration and Sabzi to Bijli, Gas, and Bachon ki Fees, with search, filters, and date-wise grouping. Income and Sadqa are tracked separately, so charity never mixes with household spending.

The Bills tab covers the monthly musts: recurring bills with due dates and an "Ada Kar Diya" tick, plus Lazmi Kharchay — fixed monthly expenses like school fees, tuition, van fare, house rent, and milk. Save them once with ready-made preset chips, then when the salary arrives, one big "Tankhwah Aa Gayi" button adds them all in a single tap, with duplicate protection so nothing is ever added twice.

Reports turn numbers into insight: month-over-month comparison, category bars, daily averages, your biggest expense, and four weeks of savings bars. Settings lets you adjust the budget and set monthly and weekly savings goals with progress tracking.

Technically, Ghar Kharcha is a single self-contained HTML file — no backend, no login, no internet needed after loading. All data stays privately in your phone's browser via localStorage. Just open index.html or host it on GitHub Pages and it works.
